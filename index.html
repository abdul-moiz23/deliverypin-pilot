Questions sellers ask

Customers won't bother with a map.

It's one tap: "Use my current location". It appears right when they hit checkout, and there's a manual address option, so no one is blocked. Capture is optional, so not every order will have a pin, and the dashboard shows you how many do.

My courier doesn't support this.

No courier integration is needed. The rider scans a QR on the label with any phone camera. PostEx label PDFs are supported today. For other couriers, print QR slips, or send us a sample label and we add the format.

Is it hard to set up?

Install it, then turn it on in Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds. No code.

What about customer privacy?

Location is only requested after the customer chooses it. There's no tracking, no data sold and nothing sent to couriers. Retention and deletion controls are built in.

Will it slow the store down?

The popup loads only when the customer goes to checkout, and the address details appear before the optional landmark lookup.

Can't we just ask for a WhatsApp location?

You can, for every order, by hand, and then copy it onto each parcel. DeliveryPin collects it at checkout and prints it on the label as a QR.

Do the brands need Shopify Plus?

No. The map popup works on every Shopify plan. DeliveryPin is for Shopify stores only.
